## 6. Substep & Timescale Limits
*   The game runs at a fixed  logic tick.
*   Macro celestial rails run at 30x time scale.
*   If the render frame drops, the physics engine may run a maximum of 5 substeps per frame. If it still falls behind, it must shed load (drop meteor visuals, freeze distant physics) rather than dilating the timestep.
